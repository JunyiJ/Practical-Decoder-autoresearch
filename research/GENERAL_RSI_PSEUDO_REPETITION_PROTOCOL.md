This freezes the design in GENERAL_RSI_PSEUDO_REPETITION_NEXT.md after the new
loss/sampler/complete-restart integration passed. The earlier native-horizon,
composer-FP32 and diagnostic studies remain closed. Their evidence motivates
this new hypothesis; none of their scores enters this confirmation.

All six runs are new continuations. Slots0/1 use native-horizon slot01 time-EMA,
slots2/3 use slot03, and slots4/5 use slot05. Within each pair run ordinary CE
then alternating CE/pseudo repetition, starting with CE. Reset optimizer and EMA
for both arms, initial LR0.00025, final-third linear cooldown to0.1, synchronized
training budget1,200 seconds each. Preserve time-EMA half-life127.184813977s,
RAW secondary, context128 and every inherited model/optimizer policy. Fresh roles,
parent receipts and deterministic order are frozen in plan.json before launch.
This is adaptation conditional on selected parents, not fresh pretraining.

Use the qualified uniform punctuation-bounded span inventory, detached previous
copy probability, lambda0.5, complete copies and masked padding. Preserve the
new sampler independently from the ordinary CE stream. Equal time changes natural
token exposure. Report CE updates, pseudo updates and valid penalty targets
separately. Heuristic spans and their source weighting differ from DITTO sentences.
No quality gate uses engineering losses, arithmetic agreement or saturation counts.
Nonfinite state remains an execution failure requiring preserved evidence.

All108 validation documents/100,416 targets are scored on RAW and final time-EMA.

before resampling and divide by resampled target counts. Bounds are conditional
on these documents and three parent/continuation pairs.

Two attempts maximum per slot,2,400 total worker seconds per slot,14,400 across

The sole scientific question is whether this complete targeted adaptation recipe
improves validation prediction at this incremental budget. Fewer loops with
inconclusive CE is useful diagnostic evidence, not validated overall improvement.
